NAFNet: real validation data vs. an unfair synthetic test

First pass ran Lenna through flat Gaussian noise and blur — and the noise result looked broken, restoration barely differed from the noisy input. Investigating why led to the real cause below, confirmed against actual held-out validation samples.

checkpoint model_nafnet_my_run.pt arch NAFNet width=48, 15.9M params val loss (best so far) 0.0853, epoch 3/30
Root cause: the training data's noise is speckle (multiplicative), with std that scales from ~0.06 in dark regions up to ~0.16 in bright regions — measured directly off data/train. The synthetic test used flat additive Gaussian noise at σ=0.15 everywhere, layered on top of a natural photograph instead of a semiconductor wafer pattern. That's two mismatches stacked at once (wrong noise statistics and wrong image domain), not a broken checkpoint. The real-data section below runs the same checkpoint on data it was actually trained for.
